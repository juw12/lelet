# Launch day: send the invitations

Today is Saturday 28 November 2026, the first day of the 14-day launch test. The counting is automatic.
The one thing a script cannot do is ask people you know. That part is yours, and it takes about 20 minutes.

## Before 10:00

- [ ] Open https://juw12.github.io/lelet/?count=off once on each of your own devices, so you are not counted as a tester.
- [ ] Run one test on your phone. If it fails, do not send anything; look at the open issues first.

## At 10:00: send 30 messages, one by one

Not a broadcast and not a group post. A message to one person gets an answer; a broadcast gets ignored.
